**TL;DR from the official docs:**

- **Import a sound clip:** in the Timeline, select the audio track → **File → Import → Sound Clip** (or right-click the track → *Import Sound Clips*). You can also drag `.wav`/`.aif` files straight from Finder/Explorer onto an audio track. Avoid long `.mp3`s — they can drift out of sync.
- **Play audio:** sound is off by default — click the **Sound** button in the Playback toolbar or choose **Play → Audio Playback**, then play the animatic.
